FAQ

Questions, answered


Who can apply?

Students and fresh graduates interested in gaining practical experience. Beginners are welcome.

How long is the internship?

Each virtual internship runs for six weeks.

Will I receive a certificate?

Yes. Completing the tasks earns a certificate with a unique verification ID.

Do I need to attend at fixed times?

Mostly no. It is remote and flexible, with optional live sessions.

How do I verify a certificate?

Enter the certificate ID on the verification page of the website.
